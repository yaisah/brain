# Provenance and acknowledgments

Brain was created by Yaisah Escobar as a personal toolkit for organizing
knowledge, goals, and workflows, independently of an employer project.

## Design inspiration

The design draws inspiration from:

- [Paweł Huryn's PM Brain](https://github.com/phuryn/pm-brain) and
  [PM Skills](https://github.com/phuryn/pm-skills): product-management workflows,
  structured context, and maintaining useful knowledge through ordinary files.
- [Andrej Karpathy's LLM Wiki](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f):
  preserving sources and maintaining linked, synthesized knowledge with an LLM.

These acknowledgments describe design influences. Brain is independently
maintained and is not affiliated with or endorsed by those creators.

## Runtime dependencies

Optional libraries are installed separately, not vendored: openpyxl and python-pptx.
Review their licenses at the versions listed in `requirements-artifacts.txt` when
preparing a release. Python, Codex, Claude Code, office viewers, and connectors are
separate products with their own licenses, accounts, and terms.
