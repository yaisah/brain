# Build a competitive battlecard

## Resolve scope

Find the nearest enclosing `.brain` marker from this skill and read that toolkit's `docs/OPERATING_GUIDE.md` before private data. Follow its single-workspace routing with `brain.py resolve`; this workflow does not expand a selected workspace into the whole brain.

`TOOLKIT_ROOT` and `BRAIN` both mean the main `Brain/` folder; `PROJECT` is the resolved workspace under its `Projects/`. Shared resource paths below are toolkit-relative; unqualified knowledge and output paths are workspace-relative. Follow `docs/OPERATING_GUIDE.md` for shared evidence, central integrations, maintenance paths, and application access.

Help a specific audience discuss a competitor or alternative accurately. Include manual work, an internal build, and doing nothing when those are the real alternatives.

## Establish the comparison

Identify the named alternative, customer segment, use case, audience, and decision being supported.
Read selected project positioning, product capabilities, customer evidence, and existing competitive notes. Treat marketing claims as claims from their source, not independently verified facts.
If current external research is requested and supported, use only the project's authorized tools and record URLs and retrieval dates. Otherwise state the date and limits of the local evidence.
Do not access another project's customer or competitor information to fill gaps.

## Compare on relevant dimensions

Choose dimensions that affect the target user's decision: workflow fit, outcome, implementation, integration, usability, reliability, service, pricing, or switching cost as relevant.
For each dimension, distinguish verified capability, source claim, reasonable inference, and unknown.
Represent the alternative's real strengths and the conditions where it is the better fit. Do not manufacture a weakness to fill a comparison table.
Keep feature presence distinct from quality of implementation and from measured customer impact.

## Draft the card

Include:

- A short positioning summary for this audience and use case.
- Where each option is a strong fit and a poor fit.
- The alternative's strengths and a truthful response grounded in the project's value.
- Common objections, concise responses, and the evidence supporting them.
- Questions that uncover fit without steering the customer through misleading premises.
- Proof points with sources and dates, plus claims requiring verification.

Avoid unsupported superiority statements, invented customer quotations, and stale prices presented as current.
When pricing is uncertain, compare structure and required validation rather than asserting a false exact total.

## Deliver and maintain

Save `outputs/YYYY-MM-DD_battlecard_<alternative>.md`. Mark the intended audience and evidence cutoff date.
Flag claims whose freshness could change the recommendation, and name the smallest research step needed.
The default artifact is an internal draft. Creating it does not authorize sending it to customers, publishing it, or contacting the competitor.
